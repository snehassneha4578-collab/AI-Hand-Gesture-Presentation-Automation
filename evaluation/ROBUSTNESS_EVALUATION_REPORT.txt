6. FAILURE CASES AND LIMITATIONS
======================================================================

1. Moderate calibration-to-live domain shift was observed.
2. TWO showed the strongest feature distribution shift.
3. Recognition performance depends on the user's live calibration.
4. Pure live-centroid results are within-session measurements.
5. The system has not yet established broad unseen-user generalization.
6. Conservative safety thresholds can reduce command coverage.
7. Lighting, distance, hand orientation, background, and user variation
   can affect landmark distributions and recognition.
8. Further cross-user and cross-environment testing is required before
   making generalized accuracy claims.
